ISCIz · Round 02 · Ledger direction locked

The Ledger, fleshed out.

Round 02 is six screens wired together: Dashboard, Detail (read-only), Edit, Create, and now Admin (Clients, Agencies, Users). Status is gone. Fuzzy search covers every field, Cmd-K opens a quick palette, rows sort/filter, and the density toggle lives in the toolbar. Click any thumbnail to jump into the live screen, then navigate between the rest from there.
